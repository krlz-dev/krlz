As a Senior Software Engineer at Portavita, I helped shape a new digital-health platform and modernize its supporting systems.

I selected FHIR HAPI and Spring Boot for the backend stack and built Python tooling to generate FHIR resources. The tooling accelerated implementation while keeping resource generation consistent.

I integrated backend services with legacy systems through Kafka and contributed to React and Next.js product flows, including OpenID registration and medical-examination workflows.
